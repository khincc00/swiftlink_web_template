Our story

From a small delivery team to a full-service transport partner

SwiftLink Logistics & Transport Pte Ltd started with a simple idea: transport in Singapore should be dependable, clearly priced and easy to arrange. What began as a small team running local deliveries has grown into a licensed, fully insured provider serving businesses, event organisers and families across the island.

Today we support goods delivery, corporate charter, point-to-point transfers and short-term warehousing, all coordinated by one team that is available 24 hours a day, including weekends and public holidays.

Our approach has not changed since day one: plan carefully, communicate clearly and deliver on time.

Our journey

Milestones along the way

  1. 2014

    SwiftLink is founded

    Started in Singapore with a small team of drivers and delivery vans serving local businesses.

  2. 2016

    Corporate charter launched

    Added executive vehicles and coaches for meetings, conferences and corporate events.

  3. 2019

    Warehouse support added

    Began offering short-term storage and distribution for retail and F&B clients.

  4. 2021

    24/7 dispatch operations

    Moved to round-the-clock operations, including weekends and public holidays.
