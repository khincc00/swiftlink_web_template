    FAQ

    Answers before you book.

    Search or filter the most common questions. Still unsure? Dispatch is on call 24/7.

    Can you do same-day or urgent jobs?

    Yes. Urgent jobs can usually be picked up within 30 to 55 minutes depending on the region and vehicle. Same-day bookings within 3 hours carry a 20% urgency surcharge, shown in your quote.

    How is the price calculated?

    Goods jobs are priced per trip by vehicle size and distance between regions. Charter and transfers are priced per hour with a minimum booking. Add-ons such as extra movers or tail-lift vehicles are itemised. The online figure is indicative; dispatch confirms a fixed written quotation.

    Do you charge more at night, on Sundays or public holidays?

    Pickups between 22:00 and 07:00 carry a 25% after-hours surcharge, and Sundays or public holidays carry 15%. Both are applied automatically in the instant quote so there are no surprises.

    Are my goods insured during the move?

    Yes. Standard goods-in-transit cover is included on every job. For high-value items you can add enhanced cover for declared values up to S$20,000. SwiftLink is LTA-licensed under licence no. LTA/TS/2023/01987.

    How do I track my booking?

    Your confirmation includes a tracking number such as SL-240918. Enter it on the Track page to see live status, driver and vehicle details and the ETA. Proof of delivery with photos and signature appears there on completion.

    Which vehicle do I need?

    Use the vehicle matcher on the Services page: enter your cartons, pallets or passengers and it recommends the smallest vehicle that fits, so you don't pay for space you don't use.

    Can I set up monthly billing for my company?

    Yes. A business account gives you 30-day credit terms, monthly statements, contract rates for recurring routes and a dedicated coordinator. It's free to open.

    What happens if something is damaged?

    Pickup and drop-off photos are taken on every job. Report any issue within 48 hours and our team handles the claim with the insurer directly, keeping you updated until it's resolved.

    How do I cancel or reschedule?

    Contact dispatch by phone, WhatsApp or email. Changes made more than 24 hours before pickup are free. Later changes may incur a fee depending on whether the vehicle has already been dispatched.
