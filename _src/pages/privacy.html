1. Who we are

This website is operated by {{c:name}} (UEN {{c:uen}}), an LTA-licensed transport operator (licence no. {{c:licence}}) with its registered office at {{c:address}} ("SwiftLink", "we", "us").

This policy explains how we handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). It applies to our website, our quote and contact forms, phone and WhatsApp enquiries, and the services we provide.

2. Personal data we collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, company, job title, email address and phone number.
  • Booking details: pickup and drop-off addresses, dates and times, a description of the goods, passenger names and numbers, and flight details for airport transfers.
  • Recipient and site contacts that you give us so we can complete a delivery.
  • Proof of delivery: photos taken at pickup and drop-off, the recipient's name and e-signature.
  • Communications: emails, WhatsApp messages, form submissions and notes of phone calls.
  • Job applications: CV, work history and driving licence details.
  • Website data: technical information such as browser type, device and pages visited, if analytics is enabled (see section 10).

Please do not send us sensitive data, such as NRIC numbers or health information, unless we specifically ask for it for a legal or safety reason.

3. How we use your personal data

  • To prepare quotations and answer enquiries.
  • To confirm, plan and carry out bookings, including assigning drivers and vehicles.
  • To send booking confirmations, tracking updates and proof of delivery.
  • To invoice, collect payment and manage business accounts.
  • To handle feedback, damage reports and insurance claims.
  • To assess job applications.
  • To keep our website secure and improve our services.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory, tax and licensing obligations.
  • To send you service news and offers, only if you have agreed (see section 11).

4. Consent and withdrawing it

By submitting a form, booking a service or contacting us, you consent to us using your personal data for the purposes above. If you give us someone else's details, such as a recipient or passenger, you confirm that they have agreed to this.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer (section 12). We will tell you what that means for any service in progress. For example, we cannot complete a delivery without the recipient's address.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where needed for the purposes above, with:

  • our drivers, movers and, where necessary, partner transport operators who carry out your job;
  • insurers, brokers and loss adjusters, when handling a claim;
  • service providers who host our website, process forms, send email or messages, or provide analytics, under contracts that require them to protect your data;
  • professional advisers such as auditors and lawyers;
  • government agencies and authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Transfers outside Singapore

Some of our service providers, such as cloud email and form services, may store data outside Singapore. When this happens, we take steps to make sure the data receives a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law. For example, invoices and accounting records are kept for at least five years, and proof-of-delivery photos for 12 months after the job. After that, data is deleted or anonymised.

8. How we protect it

We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, restricted access to booking systems and staff training. No system is completely secure, so please contact us immediately if you think your data with us has been compromised.

9. Access and correction

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, how it has been used in the past year, or ask us to correct it. Send your request to our Data Protection Officer. We aim to reply within 30 days and may charge a reasonable fee for an access request, which we will tell you about first.

10. Cookies and analytics

Our website works without cookies. Your theme preference is passed between pages in the web address and is not stored.

If we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, it sets cookies that collect information such as pages visited and device type. It does not tell us who you are. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

11. Marketing messages

We only send marketing messages by email, SMS, WhatsApp or phone if you have agreed to receive them, and in line with the Do Not Call provisions of the PDPA. You can opt out at any time using the link in the message or by contacting us.
